Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you visit our website or get in touch.

Last updated 23 November 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Celtana (pvt) Ltd (“Celtana”, “we”, “us”). For privacy questions or requests, email info@celtana.io.

Information we receive

When you visit the site, our hosting and security providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser, pages requested, and the time of your visit. This site does not have an account, contact form, or payment form.

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 and PostHog receive page views and technical details such as your browser and the page visited. PostHog also receives clicks on booking links. We do not send booking form contents to these services.

If you allow marketing, the Meta Pixel records page views and visits to service pages for ad measurement and possible future audience creation. Meta may associate this activity with your Meta account.

If you choose to book a call through Calendly, we receive the details you provide for that booking, such as your name, contact details, and anything you include in your message.

If you subscribe, Stripe handles payment information and we receive details needed to manage billing and your subscription. If you communicate with us on WhatsApp, we receive the contact details and messages you share there.

How we use it

We use technical information to deliver and protect the website and understand which pages and booking links are useful. We use booking and WhatsApp details to arrange calls, answer enquiries, and work with you. We use billing details to manage subscriptions and payments. We do not sell your personal information.

Services used by this site

Cloudflare helps deliver and protect the site. Google Fonts supplies the site’s typeface, so your browser may contact Google when a page loads. Google Analytics and PostHog provide analytics when enabled. Meta provides ad measurement when enabled. Calendly handles bookings, Stripe handles subscription payments, and WhatsApp handles messages exchanged there. These providers have their own privacy practices and may process information in other countries.

Cookies

Google Analytics may set analytics cookies if you allow analytics. Meta may set marketing cookies if you allow marketing. PostHog is configured for cookieless analytics without browser storage or session recording. Neither optional category loads before you consent. Cloudflare may use cookies needed for security. Calendly may use cookies on its own site when you follow a booking link. You can change your choice using “Cookie choices” in the footer.

Retention and your choices

We keep enquiry and communication details while needed to respond or manage a working relationship. We keep billing records as required for accounting and legal obligations. Our service providers keep technical records according to their own retention settings. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete information we hold about you, subject to applicable law, by emailing info@celtana.io.

Changes

We may update this policy when the website or our practices change. The date above shows when it was last revised.
