What kind of businesses do you work with?

Small and growing businesses that want a clearer brand, better marketing or less manual work. We can start with one focused project or channel.

How quickly will we see results?

We start by finding where leads, time or money are being lost and agree on what to measure. Some fixes can help quickly; customer growth depends on your sales cycle and the channels we use, so we review progress every month.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. Subscriptions are billed through Stripe, and you can pause or cancel at any time. There is no three-month minimum or 30-day notice.

Do you manage ad spend for us?

Yes. Media budgets are billed directly to your accounts, so you keep full ownership and visibility. Our fee covers strategy, creative and management.

How do we communicate day to day?

We use WhatsApp for quick updates and schedule review calls based on your plan. Your monthly report shows what's done and what comes next.

What happens on the strategy call?

Thirty minutes on your goals, current channels and biggest blockers. You'll leave with a few practical ideas, whether we work together or not.
